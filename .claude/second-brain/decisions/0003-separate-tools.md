# 0003: The levelling hoe and the probe are new items; the game's hoe stays as it is

**Context.** The improved hoe could be a new item or new pieces in the game's own hoe menu.

**Decision.** (owner, 2026-11-03) Separate items with their own recipes: the RcinelLevel hoe
(right hand, levels to a preset height with straight 90° walls) and the probe (off hand).

**Alternatives.** *New modes on the game's hoe*: nothing new to craft, but every player with the mod
would see changed hoe menus, and other mods that add hoe pieces would share the same menu.

**Consequences.** Recipes, crafting station, models and names have to be chosen (open in
[[rcinellevel]]). Being our own items, they can't break the game's hoe for anyone, and uninstalling
the mod only removes them. To check: what Valheim does with an item in a character's inventory or a
chest whose prefab is gone after the mod is removed, and what the README should tell players.
